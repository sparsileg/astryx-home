Collectyx Walk-through

A media collection tracker

01 — To Be Read

A ranked queue for what's next

Numbered order at top, unranked pool below, each book expandable for a synopsis pulled right into the list. Start Reading or Finished buttons move a book forward without re-typing title or author — one click carries it into your next collection.

02 — Dashboard

A seven-card overview

Reading goals pace chart, what's-next queue, recently finished, quick stats, library stats, checked-out books. Yearly statistics chart tracks books and pages across every year on record. One glance shows where you stand today and where the year is heading.

03 — Books Read

Your full reading history

Sortable and paged, with tags and star ratings visible per row. Smooth paging controls — start/prev/next/end plus slider — keep a huge log fast to browse.

04 — Search and Filter

Find one book inside thousands

Stack multiple filters — author, tag, date range, anything — and combine with live text/tag search. Result count and timestamp shown right in the status bar.

05 — My Library

Your personal catalog

Status column shows Available or checked-out-by-whom at a glance. To Read and Check Out/In actions sit right on each row — no modal needed for the common actions.

06 — Tags

Full control over your only classification axis

Every tag, usage count, sortable by name/count/last-updated. Rename or delete inline — delete offers a substitute tag so books don't go untagged.

07 — Menu

The right actions, always surfaced

Global actions — Settings, Backup, Export, Restore, Find ISBNs, Find Duplicates — plus a section that swaps to match whichever collection is open. One menu, no hunting.
