Kansha Feature List

Local, private personal finance

Accounts & the Register

  • Every kind of account — checking, savings, cash, credit cards, loans, investments, and other assets such as a house, grouped with their balances in the Accounts panel.
  • A keyboard-first register — one transaction per row with a running balance; enter, change, and move around without touching the mouse.
  • Splits and transfers — divide one transaction across several categories, and move money between accounts with both sides kept in step.
  • Filter, find, and replace — narrow a register to what you're looking for, and fix a payee or category across many transactions at once.
  • Search everything — type a payee, category, memo, check number, or amount to find every matching transaction in the book.
  • Undo — Ctrl+Z takes back the last change in a register.

Bills, Schedules & Reconciling

  • Scheduled transactions — paychecks, rent, and bills that come again and again; Kansha reminds you when one is due, or enters it by itself.
  • Calendar — see each schedule on the day it falls, with the balance it leaves behind.
  • Reconcile against statements — enter the statement's date and ending balance, tick what it shows, and finish when the difference is zero.
  • Loans with amortization — give a loan its terms and Kansha splits each payment into principal and interest, adds the payment reminder, and keeps up with rate changes and extra payments.

Investments

  • Lots and cost basis — every lot is kept with its acquisition date and cost, so capital gains come out right at tax time.
  • Lot methods that match your broker — choose how sales are matched to lots, and line Kansha up with your brokerage's records.
  • Guided investment entry — buys, sells, dividends, reinvestments, gifts of shares, and Roth conversions, each through a dialog that knows what the action needs.
  • Investments screen — every holding at today's prices, with optional price download (only ticker symbols are ever sent).

Reports, Insights & Budgets

  • Reports with drill-down — spending, net worth, and tax reports; click any amount to see the transactions behind it.
  • Saved reports and export — customize a report, save it to run again, and export it to CSV or PDF.
  • Insights home page — tabs of cards, each a small report that keeps itself up to date, including spending cards of your own.
  • Budgets — set an amount per category and month, then compare actuals in a graph view or month by month across the year.

Getting Data In

  • Quicken import — reads the QIF files Quicken exports: accounts, categories, tags, payees, securities, prices, and every transaction, with transfers matched up.
  • Test before you import — see what Kansha found before anything changes, try the import first, and take a whole import back out if needed.
  • Command-line entry — kansha add records a purchase from a terminal without opening the app.
  • Entry files — entries can arrive as files from a script or typed on a phone, and wait in a review inbox until you approve them.
  • Sample data — load three years of made-up accounts and transactions into an empty book to try things out.

Privacy & Data Safety

  • Your data stays with you — everything lives in one file on your own computer; no account needed, nothing sent anywhere unless you turn on price or currency lookups.
  • Encrypted book — the book is encrypted with your passphrase, so a lost laptop or backup drive doesn't expose your finances.
  • Automatic backups — on close, a few minutes after a change, and before anything big such as an import; each backup is one encrypted file.
  • Restore with a preview — see what you'd gain or lose before bringing a book back from a backup, and move a book to another computer the same way.
  • Integrity check and audit trail — every change is recorded, and the book can be checked for problems at any time.
  • Exact money math — amounts are stored as whole cents, never floating point, so totals always add up.

Make It Yours

  • Themes, fonts, and text size — chosen per computer, right from the menu bar.
  • Custom navigation bar — put any menu item or any account on a button, in the order you like.
  • Windows and the dock — keep several reports and screens open at once and switch between them from the dock.
  • Built-in User's Guide — Help > User's Guide opens the full manual from inside the app.
